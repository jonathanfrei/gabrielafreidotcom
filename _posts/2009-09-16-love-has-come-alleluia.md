This recording is from a wedding I recently sang for here in Florida. The bride and groom loved Matt Maher's song *Love Has Come* and wanted me to use the chorus of that song for the Alleluia in their wedding Mass. I also rewrote the Gospel Acclamation verse usually used in weddings to fit into the rhythm of the verse. Enjoy :)

**Lyrics:**

Chorus:

Word of God enthroned,

dwell in us forevermore;

Love has come to show the way.

Hallelujah, peace be with us,

Love has come to show the way.

Verse:

All those who now live in love are united with their God,

For they live in God and God lives in them! (Chorus)
