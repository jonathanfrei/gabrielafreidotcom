A friend of mine, Heather Bartlett, asked me to record this beautiful song. It's called "You Are Loved (Don't Give Up)," off of Josh Groban's album Awake. It's one of my favorite songs that he has recorded, and will forever remind me of my summer abroad.

The message is clear: never give in to despair or loneliness, because someone loves you. No matter who you are or what you have done, you are loved, so never give up.

I retain NO rights to this song. It is the property of Josh Groban and his recording label. Words & Music by Thomas Salter.

Enjoy :)
