Lord, i've learned a few lessons today: help me to do better tomorrow.

Lord, i've conquered some fears today: help me be brave tomorrow.

Lord, i've swallowed some pride today: help me be humble tomorrow.

Lord, i've hurt you today: help me love you tomorrow.

Lord, i give you my anxiety today: help me be peaceful tomorrow.

Lord, i give you my imperfection today: help me see the good things tomorrow.

Lord, i give you my entire heart today: help me receive a little more of you tomorrow.
