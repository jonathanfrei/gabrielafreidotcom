Matt Maher has a beautiful arrangement of the Agnus Dei, or Lamb of God. This is a live performance of that Lamb of God from a wedding here in Florida.

Special thanks to Anders Bergmann, one of the groomsmen, for singing the duet part with me!

**Lyrics**:

Lamb of God (Lamb of God),

You take away (You take away)

The sin of all (the sin of all)

The world (the world).

Misere nobis (Misere nobis). -- *translation: Have mercy on us.* (Repeat)

Agnus Dei (Agnus Dei) -- *translation: Lamb of God*

You take away (You take away)

The sin of all (the sin of all)

The world (the world).

Dona nobis pacem (Dona nobis pacem). -- *translation: Grant us peace.*

Words & Music by Matt Maher
