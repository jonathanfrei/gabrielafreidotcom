This is an original song that I wrote a few months after I began dating my husband, Jonathan. I had never dated anyone before him. I made a commitment to wait to date a person that I honestly could see myself marrying someday.  It took me until college to find that right person to start dating, but God was good to me and I married that man three years later.

I wrote "Love Song" specifically to be performed at weddings, and modeled it after the vows couples profess to each other. This is actually the short version of "Love Song." There is a third verse and final chorus. I hope to have a video up soon of the full version, but wanted you to be able to enjoy this for now.

**Lyrics:**

~*verse one*~

Darling, I waited this time to find you.

I looked, I searched, and still I was alone.

At the moment I least expected, God brought you to my side.

He opened my eyes to see you and you took my breath away.

~*chorus*~

Now here we are in this moment together.

You have my heart. Don’t ever doubt, I love you.

You’re my gift from God, my priceless treasure, the beloved of my heart.

And I just can’t wait to spend the rest of my life with you.

~*verse two*~

Darling, I say “For better, for worse” and I mean it.

I place my hand in yours, and two become one.

Though I never could have dreamed it, God fashioned me for you.

His perfect plan is realized, all my hopes and dreams came true.

We’ll face the future with our Lord and God before us.
