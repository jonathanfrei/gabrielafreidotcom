**Inspiration:**

This song, to this day, is one of the most powerful for me personally. Every time I play it, I realize how much I need to hear the words, to be reminded of the fact that I am helpless without the strength and grace of the Lord. “There are things about myself I cannot change alone.” The Lord waits for us to fall to our knees and surrender our imperfections to Him, willing to live our lives carrying that particular cross if it is His will. Only in such state of surrender can He truly step in and heal.
