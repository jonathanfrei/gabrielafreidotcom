**Inspiration:**

This song was inspired by a pilgrimage to the Holy Land that my family was blessed to make in 2005. We had the incredible opportunity to walk where Jesus walked, and to visit so many places that were familiar to Him on this earth.

I was struck by the incredible symmetry between Our Lord and His Mother. We visited the Basilica of the Annunciation and the Garden of Gethsemane, and I realized the beautiful union of the two Hearts, the Sacred and the Most Sorrowful and Immaculate, in saying “yes” to the will of the Father. My own prayer is to grow in surrender, to be able, with His grace, to say Fiat – Let it be done to me.
